What's in the city

Everything is built for this server, so it all fits together: one menu, one look, and systems that talk to each other.

01

Jobs and paydays

Twelve jobs across citizens, vendors, police and crime. Every job pays on a timer, so you're never broke for long.

02

Elections and laws

Win the vote to become Mayor, write the laws, call lockdowns, approve warrants and run a city lottery.

03

Crafting

Dig through dumpsters for parts, build at a crafting table, or sell what you find to the Fence. No questions asked.

04

Duels

Bet on a fair fight in the arena with the Duel Master. Same gear for both sides, winner takes the pot.

05

Courier runs

Take a timed job from the Courier Boss and race packages to dead drops across the map before the clock runs out.

06

City events

Some nights the city goes wrong. Survive Zombie Night with everyone else, or profit from the chaos.

07

Property

Buy doors, lock up, title your place and hand out keys. Raids need a warrant, and the Mayor has to sign off.

VR

Optional VR

Jump in with a headset whenever you like: real hands, two-handed guns, wrist displays. Desktop players play alongside.

Jobs

12

Press F4 in game to switch. Some jobs have limited slots, and the Mayor is elected.

Citizens

Citizen
A regular person trying to make it in the city. Find a place to live and figure out how to earn your keep.
Hobo
Nothing but the clothes on your back. Ask for spare change and keep your head above water.
Medic
People get hurt, and you're the one they call. Patch them up and set your own prices.

Civil Protection

Police Officer
Keep the peace, answer 911 calls and bring wanted suspects in with your arrest baton.
Police Chief
Run the department, lead the bigger raids and make the calls when things get serious.
Mayor
The city is yours to run. Write the laws and keep the people happy, or they'll vote you out.

Vendors

Gun Dealer
Keep the city armed. Order crates, open shop and be careful who you sell to.
Contraband Dealer
Sell the things nobody's supposed to have. Keep your stock hidden and your eyes open.

Criminals

Gangster
Part of the crew. Do what the Mob Boss asks and stay a step ahead of the cops.
Mob Boss
Call the shots for the gang, keep your crew loyal and make sure nothing leads back to you.
Thief
Locked doors are just a suggestion. Get in, grab what you can carry, don't get caught.
Hacker
Bypass keypads and security systems for the right price, with a keypad cracker in your pocket.

Rules

9

Short and simple. Staff have the final say.

  1. Don't kill people for no reason. Have a roleplay reason, and give them a chance to react.
  2. If you die, forget that fight. Don't run straight back for revenge.
  3. No killing, robbing or raiding in spawn.
  4. Don't block, trap or push people around with props.
  5. Play your job. Police don't raid for fun, and medics don't rob banks.
  6. No cheats, exploits or scripts. Found a bug? Report it instead of using it.
  7. No hate speech or harassment. Keep it friendly.
  8. No spamming chat or the mic.
  9. Staff have the final say. If you disagree, talk it out calmly.

Getting started

You'll be fine. Most people will talk back if you talk to them.

F4

Pick a job

F4 opens pretty much everything: jobs, the shop, chat speech and more. Every job pays, so pick one and go.

E

Dig around

Press E on a dumpster. You might find health, ammo or crafting parts worth real money to the right person.

/

Ask the city

Read /laws before you get arrested, /help lists every command, and the Welcome NPC in spawn can point you around.
